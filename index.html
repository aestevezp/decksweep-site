DeckSweep

Swipe your Mac clean from your iPhone.

A small helper on your Mac finds old screenshots, duplicate photos, forgotten downloads and used installers. The iPhone app turns them into a deck: swipe right to keep, left to trash. Nothing is deleted — files go to your Mac's Trash, and nothing ever leaves your Mac.

Download DeckSweep for Mac macOS 14 or later · notarized

Then get the iPhone app from the App Store, open it on the same Wi‑Fi, and scan the QR code in the menu bar.

How it stays safe

  • Only the folders you choose. Never system files, never other apps' data.
  • Trash, never delete. Undo works even after committing.
  • No account, no cloud, no analytics. The phone and the Mac talk directly, encrypted, on your own network.
